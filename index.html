Dalmatian Species Survival Game

An interactive ecology project

About

This project was created by our ecology group as part of our Dalmatian Coast ecology assignment. Our goal was to make our booth more interactive by turning species research into a simple arcade-style survival game.

Why We Made It

Our professor encouraged us to find a creative way to engage visitors during the outreach event. This game was our response: a small, playable experience that helps students learn while interacting with our project.

Featured Species

  • Apollo Butterfly A butterfly affected by habitat fragmentation and environmental change.
  • Griffon Vulture A large scavenging bird impacted by habitat conditions and human infrastructure.

How to Play

Choose a species, avoid obstacles, collect Dalmatian karst stones for bonus points, and learn an ecology fact after each round.
